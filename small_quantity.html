💡 How This Advisor Helps

This tool calculates estimated profitability for different selling quantities using available market prices and your entered production and transportation costs.

Profit per kg = Selling price − Production cost − Transport cost.
Total estimated profit = Profit per kg × Selling quantity.

Selling prices are based on a downloaded government mandi data snapshot and may not represent current live prices. Production and transport costs are entered by the user. Estimated profits are not guaranteed actual earnings.
