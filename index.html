How GramVyapar AI works
Estimated profit per kg = Selling price − Production cost − Transport cost.
Total estimated profit = Estimated profit per kg × Quantity.

Data transparency: Market prices are taken from a downloaded government mandi data snapshot, not a live feed. Actual selling prices and expenses may differ. Profit figures are estimates, not guaranteed earnings.
